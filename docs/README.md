# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation repository. This collection of documents provides standardized processes, templates, and guidance for managing projects and delivering value consistently across the organization.

## Purpose

The OctoAcme project management framework centralizes scattered project knowledge into a searchable, versioned set of artifacts. This enables:
- **Equal access**: All team members have visibility into processes, decisions, and rationale
- **Consistency**: Standardized workflows and checklists reduce variability and single-person dependencies
- **Rapid onboarding**: New team members can quickly understand our approach and best practices
- **Continuous improvement**: Processes are living documents that evolve based on team feedback and learnings

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle that emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, and data-informed decisions:

1. **Initiation**: Validate business need, align stakeholders, and authorize work
2. **Planning**: Break work into shippable increments, identify risks and dependencies
3. **Execution & Tracking**: Manage day-to-day delivery, maintain quality standards, and track progress
4. **Release & Deployment**: Standardize release processes to reduce risk and improve observability
5. **Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement**: Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements

Throughout all phases, we maintain clear ownership, manage risks proactively, and communicate regularly with stakeholders.

## Key Artifacts

Throughout a project lifecycle, you'll create and maintain these key artifacts:
- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — High-level timeline and key milestones
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized, estimated work items with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Identified risks with impact, likelihood, owner, and mitigation plans
- **Release Notes** — Summary of changes, migration steps, and known issues
- **Retrospective Notes** — Learnings and action items for continuous improvement

## Core Principles

OctoAcme projects are guided by these principles:
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments frequently
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named owners for Product, Project Management, and Execution
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily standups** (15 min) — Focus on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM sync** — Alignment and prioritization
- **Twice-weekly team standups** — Delivery team coordination
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** — Progress and risk reporting
- **Sprint demos and retrospectives** — At milestone or sprint completion
